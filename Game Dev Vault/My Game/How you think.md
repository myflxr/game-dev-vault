# How you think

Back to [[My Game]] · Related: [[Working rules]]

The AI reads this **before deciding anything** on the game and adds to it whenever you decide, correct or react to something. Each point is a pattern from what you actually said or did, with an example and a date. **If something here is wrong, edit it.** Your word beats this note.

> ✏️ *The points below are examples for Pizza Rush. Delete them and let your AI fill this page in as you work: tell it "add that to How you think" whenever you make a choice.*

## What you're aiming for
- **Fun first, then pretty.** A feature that feels good with grey boxes beats a pretty one that's boring.
  *"make it work first, we'll do the art later"* (ovens, 2026-01-01)
- **Fair for free players.** Paid items save time; they never lock content.
  *"no pay to win"* (shop, 2026-01-01)

## How you decide
- **Fix the blocker first.** Don't build on something broken.
- **Small steps.** One feature finished and tested before the next one starts.

## How you like to work with the AI
- **Explain the plan in plain words first**, then build.
- **Big builds: go ahead, report once at the end.** Small fixes: just do them.
- **Ask first** about anything with players' money, saved data or deleting things.
- **Proof over promises:** say how it was tested (Play test, numbers, screenshots).
- **"you decide"** = the AI picks, says what it picked and why, and keeps it easy to change.

## Your setup
- Roblox Studio on a laptop (Studio runs slow, so judge performance on a real phone too).
- Most players are on **phones**, so every UI must work on touch.

## Not sure yet (ask when it comes up)
- How much monetisation (game passes vs. dev products)
- Art style: cartoon or realistic?

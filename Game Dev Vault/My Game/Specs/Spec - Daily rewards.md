## What I want
When you join, a popup shows a 7-day calendar. You claim today's prize. Come back tomorrow for a bigger one. Miss a day and it starts over.

## Must have
- 7 days, prize grows each day (cash)
- The **server** checks the time (players can't cheat by changing their clock)
- Works on phone
- A small red dot on the menu button when a prize is ready

## Must NOT change
- Existing saved cash and rebirths
- The shop prices

## Questions I already answered
- Reset after missing one day: **yes**
- Robux involved: **no**

## How I'll know it's done
- Day 1 claim works, popup closes, cash goes up
- Test with the clock moved forward: day 2 is bigger, day 9 after a gap starts over
